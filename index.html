About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Universidad Nacional del Sur. Currently, I serve as an Independent Researcher at CONICET (Interdisciplinary Institute of Basic Sciences - ICB CONICET UNCuyo) and a Full Professor at the Faculty of Engineering, National University of Cuyo (UNCuyo).


I lead research initiatives within the CICDA Group (Computación Inteligente y Ciencia de Datos Aplicada), focusing on Computational Intelligence, Metaheuristics, Optimization, Smart Mobility, and Medical Image Processing.
